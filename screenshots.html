The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Playbook

The Slingshot is part of a family of free, open source educational simulations and tools in innovation and entrepreneurship, which includes The Disruptor, a corporate entrepreneurship and innovation simulation played as Chief Innovation Officer, and Build, Bin, Boost, where you run a technology company’s R&D portfolio as Head of R&D. Subscribe to The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Playbook to keep up with the latest developments. Ideas and suggestions always welcome.
