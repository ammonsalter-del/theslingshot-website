The Slingshot

An AI Startup Simulation

Version 1.2 | January 2026

Can you build the next AI unicorn?

It's 2026. AI may be the defining technology of our era.

Economists call it a potential general purpose technology—like steam, electricity, or computing—capable of transforming every sector. But GPTs don't apply themselves. The value lies in identifying and exploiting opportunities: discovering solutions that weren't possible before, disrupting established industries, creating entirely new markets.

Across the UK, a new generation of startups is racing to capture this value. Some spin out from world-leading AI labs, pushing the frontier of what's possible. Others bring deep domain expertise—in healthcare, finance, logistics, creative industries—and see exactly where AI could be transformative. All face the same challenge: turning potential into profit before the window closes.

Now it's your turn. Choose your venture, assemble your team, and chase the ultimate prize: a billion-dollar unicorn.

The potential is enormous. So are the risks.
That's what makes it interesting—and that's how you'll learn.

Learn Technology Venturing Through Experience

The Slingshot teaches through experiential learning—not lectures or cases, but by putting you in the founder's seat making real decisions under uncertainty.

Every aspect of the game is connected. Decisions in one area help or hinder others. The game engine includes some luck, but rewards approaches that balance different tensions and align resources with context.

🎯 Strategic Thinking

  • Interdependencies between technology, market, and finance
  • Evaluating competing options under uncertainty
  • Balancing tensions and aligning resources
  • Stakeholder dynamics in technology commercialization

💰 Entrepreneurial Finance

  • Startup funding mechanisms and sources
  • Equity dilution and cap table management
  • Cash runway and burn rate dynamics
  • Term sheet negotiations with investors

🚀 Technology Venturing

  • Commercializing research and deep tech
  • University spin-outs and IP considerations
  • Grant applications and non-dilutive funding
  • Balancing scientific rigor with commercial pressure

💪 Execution & Resilience

  • Strategic pivoting when conditions change
  • Managing multiple concurrent priorities
  • Learning from failure and iterating
  • Autonomous decision-making under pressure

⚡ The Challenge is the Point

Most players fail their first attempt—and that's by design. The game teaches through experience, failure, and replay. Each run reveals new strategic insights.

How to Play

Getting Started

No installation required. Runs in any modern browser. Works best on desktop/laptop.

Choose between quick tutorial (~3 min) or comprehensive walkthrough (~10 min). Guided play mode available for first-time players.

Founding Your Startup

Choose your sector: 8 AI industries with unique dynamics

Select founder type: 12 backgrounds with different strengths

Pick location: 6 UK cities with distinct characteristics

Initial funding: Bootstrap, crowdfunding, or angels

Quarterly Gameplay

Allocate attention: Limited points across departments

Respond to events: 550+ realistic scenarios

Make strategic choices: Pivoting, hiring, fundraising, product development

Race competitors: 3 AI rivals pursuing same milestones

Win Conditions

Victory: Complete all three milestones before running out of cash

Your outcome: Final valuation × your ownership percentage

Learning: Most fail first attempt—that's expected and part of the process

For Educators

Innovation is often recombination. Fatboy Slim built a career on it—sampling, remixing, turning existing tracks into something entirely new. That's what we can do with teaching tools.

The Slingshot is a free, open AI startup simulation for teaching entrepreneurship—vibe coded with LLMs.

🔧 Want to Adapt It? It's Easy.

Here's the thing: it is easy. No software skills needed. And no desire to learn them. Just describe what you want and the LLMs build it.

How to Remix The Slingshot:
  1. Download the HTML file from GitHub
  2. Upload it to Claude, GPT, or Gemini
  3. Tell it what you want to change
  4. Congratulations—you're now a game developer
⚠️ One Warning: Coding is easy, debugging is hell. Budget time for hunting down the weird things that break.

Would you make pasta by growing your own wheat? Start with The Slingshot and reshape it. That's how progress works.

The code is open. Like Erdős with his proofs, this needs collaborators not followers. Someone in São Paulo might build on what you started in Stockholm.

A community of educators making better tools together, rather than each of us starting from scratch or paying for commercial simulations we can't adapt.
